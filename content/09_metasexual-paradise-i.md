![](sin-in-heaven.jpg)Should we see there, in the majority line of commentators, the image of sin, the curse of concupiscence, the shame of adultery? That would be to contradict the rest of the panel, in particular the image of the original, perfectly innocent couple, in the foreground of paradise. It is more logical to decipher the reproductive function, presented as inferior to the transcendent function of the Eros, just as the amniotic pocket and floating peach followed the two leaves of thistle. This notion of inferiority must however be understood without pejorative connotation: biologicals belong to the earthly part of Creation and the spiritual associates with a rise to heaven.

Attraction for a third person (or a third person) reminds us that triangular dynamics is still present, if only potentially. How many newlyweds have experienced it: barely the ceremony on the calendar, irresistible impulses arise that draw them to another soul mate... Arthur, Guinevere and Lancelot are probably the most famous allegory.

Bosch presents this phenomenon here as intrinsically linked to procreation. In terms of metasexuality, we can explain this by the need for an external energy supply capable to renew and enrich the circulating energy of the couple, particularly when it comes to welcoming a new soul (cf. the bearers of the mussel and blackberry in the pedagogic province). In a "normal" psyche, structured in accordance with natural laws, amorous openness poses no problem. We feel pain when ego clings to the possession of the partner, or to principles such as blood ties. In an attitude of oblation, the third relationship comes as, on the contrary, as *a gift from heaven*, as a multiplication of love, as a providential contribution of energy, and in no way a division or subtraction. I have been able to observe it many times: *love of love* is a natural, innate impulse that basically solves the problem of love suffering and leads to extrasensory connection.

The confluence of external energy is in fact indispensable to ensure the spiritual dimension of the reproductive act. For example, it brings about visions of the child's destiny, the meaning of his or her existence in relation to that of his or her parents, even indicating his or her first name. The presence of the third person is also, more prosaically, the guarantee of good acceptance of the child, of good education, of good development, as the function of godfather or godmother wanted. This notion, which was taken up and sterilized by rationalist morality, did not exclude, at a time when sexual hospitality still prevailed, erotic contacts between the various partners.

![](black-man-assault.jpg)This interpretation is confirmed in the symmetrical pattern, on the diagonal passing through the siren and the center of the sphere: a young blonde woman, sitting in a very romantic little gondola, is surprised by the advances of a man of color. ![](baie-rouge-vegetale.jpg)The solution to the dilemma is given by the motif from the grieved swimmer emerges from. Indeed, the simple fact of discovering that love impulses are not in themselves biased toward possessing one partner over another, but toward a transcendent energy, upends the situation. Possession becomes meaningless, the fear of losing the object of love evaporates as if by miracle. The perception of this transcendent energy, the fullness with which true love magic gratifies us, naturally leads us to respect the impulses of the third person, as well as the impulses *for* him/her: the more sources of energy, the more exchanges and enrichment possible, therefore *more bliss*. Easier also becomes the access to archetypes.

Many spouses found, most often without understanding the secret, or even completely ignoring their partner's indiscretions, a sudden increase in the delights of love, as if adultery revived a worn-out relationship. The phenomenon is perfectly explained by the supply of external energy, capable of reviving exchanges within the couple. This is the same principle that *swingers* or the followers of polyamory, try to implement, most often without any means to escape from the obstacles which are the search for pleasure and mentalization, which nip in the bud any embryo of magic.

Let us now try to decipher the mysterious constructions Bosch placed on the four arms of paradise waters. The two most extreme, to the uttermost left and right, are labelled by Fraenger as female and male rocks.

![](rose-tubes-formation.jpg)To the left, at the feet of the female sex rock, emergesa small group of riders, coming out of the base with vaginal appearances, as if to set out to conquer the world. Their gesture expresses expectation, as if something ought to fall for them. If you follow their eyes, you come right to one of them that offers a red spherule as a pasture to a large golden fish. A dolphin is hung between the two branches of an almost vertical branch, where another red spherule sticks.

Experience showed this energy can generate visions foretelling future parents of an opportunity of procreation. It is also responsible for giving coitus an overpowering magical dimension, which proves its inspiration, with the risk of fixating the spouses to the point that they will try to repeat it while this time refusing conception and losing access to the sacred Eros. *Repetitiveness (eight- or twelve-legged crocodiles) destroys transcendence*, creates frustration and obsessive desire and generates aggressive impulses most often leading to the ruin of the couple.

It starts with slight impatience, intolerance to the partner's faults, then crystallizes itself in reproaches, discontents, more and more scathing words, sometimes culminating in household scenes, broken dishes if not worse. Hence the importance of deactivating the mind and letting subtle energies (blue sphere) to direct sexuality. Only love magic avoids repetition and organizes sexual relations, especially with third parties, so as to nourish the creativity and bliss of the partners and, through their influence, those of the family or the group.
