Cover: first decryption keys
Front: Excerpt from the first panel of the *Garden of Earthly Delights, the Creator draws attention to the importance of the initiation that the triptych will impart.
Back: Excerpt from the central panel showing that there are two different forms of love: one that leads to procreation (mold and amniotic sac), and the other to a nourishing energy of a spiritual nature (giant blackberry).
Bottom of Back: Excerpt from the right panel, morality that enters through the ears leads to sexual obsession (bagpipes), the loss of inner unity (ruptured egg), and a false wisdom (key) that imprisons the faithful.
