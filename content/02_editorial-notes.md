title: "Editorial Notes"
weight: 2
---

This book offers, for the first time in 500 years, a comprehensive and coherent interpretation of Hieronymus Bosch's three most famous triptychs: The *Garden of Earthly Delights*, The *Haywain Tryptich*, and The Temptation of Saint Anthony. Unlike conventional exegetes, the author draws on his own concrete observations of the relationship between love and extrasensory abilities. He reveals in the Flemish painter's work a prophetic analysis of the disenchantment of our modern society, denouncing both the lies of religion and materialistic illusions. Readers will also find unexpected solutions to the everyday problems of relationships, love, and spirituality.
